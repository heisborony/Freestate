*Freestate is a software pricing intelligence platform that tells you what “free” actually means. It analyzes software products, their free tiers, limitations, paid plans, pricing, and pricing changes, then classifies each product as Free, Freemium, Bait, or Paid. Freestate focuses on practical usability rather than marketing claims, helping users understand whether a product can genuinely be used without paying. It provides pricing summaries, evidence from official sources, pricing history, and relevant alternatives. Starting as a browser extension, Freestate can evolve into a comprehensive software pricing database and, ultimately, a broader pricing intelligence platform for consumers and businesses.**
